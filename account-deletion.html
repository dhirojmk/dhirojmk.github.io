Account Deletion Request

If you no longer want to use Habito, you can request deletion of your Habito account and associated account data.

Habito currently processes account deletion requests manually to help ensure that the correct account and associated data are deleted.

Your request is important to us.
We aim to complete account deletion requests within 30 days after receiving and verifying your request.

How to Request Account Deletion

1
Use your registered email address

Send the request from the email address associated with your Habito account.

2
Send an account deletion request

Send an email requesting deletion of your Habito account and associated data.

3
We verify your request

We may verify that the request was submitted by the owner of the Habito account before processing the deletion.

4
Your account is deleted

After verification, we will delete the Habito account and associated account data from our applicable systems.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

Once your deletion request has been verified, we will delete account-associated information that is stored for your Habito account, subject to any information that we are legally required or permitted to retain.

Depending on your use of Habito, this may include:

  • Habito account information
  • Firebase Authentication account information
  • Email address associated with the account
  • Display name associated with the account
  • Habits and routines associated with the account
  • Habit completion history
  • Goals and schedules
  • Habit progress and streak information
  • XP, points, and achievement information
  • Account-associated preferences and application data

Deletion Timeline

We aim to process verified account deletion requests within 30 days.

In some cases, deletion may be completed sooner.

If additional verification or information is required, we may contact you using the email address associated with your request.

Limited legal retention

Certain information may need to be retained where required or permitted by applicable law, security requirements, fraud prevention requirements, dispute resolution, or other legitimate legal obligations.

Any information that must be retained will be limited to the applicable purpose and retained only for the period reasonably necessary.

What Happens After Deletion?

Once your account deletion request has been completed, your Habito account will no longer be available for normal account access.

Account-associated data stored in our applicable cloud services will be deleted as part of the deletion process, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.

Data that exists only on your device may remain on the device until you remove the Habito application or clear its local application data.

Important Information

  • Account deletion is permanent and cannot normally be undone.
  • You should make sure that you want to permanently delete your Habito account before submitting a request.
  • Please submit the request using your registered account email address.
  • We may request additional information if we need to verify ownership of the account.
  • Account deletion does not automatically delete information that you may have independently shared outside Habito.
