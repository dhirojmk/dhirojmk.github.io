Habito – Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Habito – Daily Habit Tracker

Effective Date: October 3, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Habito – Daily Habit Tracker ("Habito", "we", "our", or "us").

Habito is a habit tracking and daily routine application designed to help users create habits, organize routines, set goals, receive reminders, track progress, maintain streaks, and improve daily consistency.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, stored, used, processed, and protected when you use Habito.

By using Habito, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Habito may process the following categories of information.

2.1 Account Information

Habito may use Firebase Authentication and Google Sign-In to allow users to create and access an account.

Depending on the authentication method used, this may include:

  • Email address
  • Display name
  • Firebase user ID
  • Authentication provider information
  • Profile information made available by the authentication provider

Firebase Authentication may also process technical information such as IP address, user-agent information, Firebase Android App ID, and related information used to provide authentication, security, and abuse prevention.

2.2 Habit and Routine Information

When you use Habito, you may create and store information such as:

  • Habit names
  • Routine names and information
  • Categories
  • Activities and sub-categories
  • Daily, weekly, or monthly goals
  • Target values
  • Priority settings
  • Reminder and alarm settings
  • Habit completion information
  • Streak information
  • Progress information
  • XP, points, levels, and achievements
  • Theme and personalization settings

2.3 Device and App Information

Firebase services used by Habito may process certain technical information required to provide and maintain Firebase functionality.

Depending on the Firebase services enabled and how they are used, this may include:

  • Firebase installation identifier
  • Firebase Android App ID
  • Application package name
  • Application and SDK version information
  • Android operating system information
  • Device and platform information
  • Language information
  • Country information
  • Time zone information
  • IP address where required by Firebase services

3. How We Use Information

Information processed by Habito and its service providers may be used for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing user accounts
  • Authenticating users
  • Synchronizing application data where cloud synchronization is enabled
  • Saving and retrieving habits and routines
  • Tracking habit completion and progress
  • Calculating streaks, XP, points, and levels
  • Providing reminders and alarms
  • Providing application functionality
  • Maintaining application security
  • Preventing abuse and unauthorized access
  • Configuring application features
  • Maintaining and improving Firebase services used by Habito

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Firebase Authentication

Habito uses Firebase Authentication to support user authentication and account management.

If you sign in using Google or another supported authentication provider, information made available by that provider may be used to create and manage your Habito account.

Firebase Authentication may process information such as your email address, display name, user ID, IP address, user-agent information, and application identifiers depending on the authentication method and Firebase configuration.

Authentication information is used to securely authenticate users, maintain sessions, protect accounts, and provide account-related functionality.

5. Cloud Firestore

Habito may use Firebase Cloud Firestore to store and synchronize application data associated with your account.

Depending on the features you use, Firestore data may include habits, routines, completion history, goals, schedules, progress, preferences, and other information generated through your use of Habito.

Firestore data is associated with your account where authentication is used and is processed to provide application functionality and synchronization.

Firebase encrypts Firebase data in transit using HTTPS.

6. Firebase Remote Config

Habito uses Firebase Remote Config to remotely configure certain application behavior and settings.

Firebase Remote Config may automatically process information such as country code, language code, time zone, Android operating system version, platform version, Firebase Android App ID, package name, and Remote Config SDK version.

This information is used to provide and maintain Remote Config functionality and to deliver appropriate configuration values.

7. Local Storage

Habito also uses local Android storage technologies, including Room and DataStore, for application functionality.

Information may be stored locally on your device for purposes such as maintaining application state, preferences, cached information, scheduled reminders, and other functionality.

Local data is protected by the security mechanisms provided by the Android operating system and your device.

8. Notifications and Alarms

Habito may schedule notifications and alarms based on the habits and reminder times you configure.

Notification and alarm functionality is used only to provide reminders and other application features that you request.

Habito does not need access to your contacts, SMS messages, phone calls, microphone, camera, or location to provide these reminder features.

9. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Based on the current functionality of Habito, the application does not require access to:

  • Precise location
  • Approximate location
  • Contacts
  • SMS messages
  • Phone call history
  • Microphone recordings
  • Camera images
  • Photos and videos
  • Financial or payment information
  • Government identification documents

Habito also does not intentionally sell personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

10. Data Sharing

Habito does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Information may be processed by Firebase and other service providers that are necessary to provide authentication, cloud storage, configuration, security, and related application functionality.

Such service providers process information according to their applicable terms and privacy practices and based on the services configured by the developer.

We may disclose information where required by applicable law, legal process, court order, governmental request, or where reasonably necessary to protect the security and rights of users, the application, or others.

11. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed by Habito against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Data transmitted to Firebase services is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.

Authentication and cloud data are protected using the security controls provided by Firebase and the application's Firebase security configuration.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and account credentials.

No electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Data Retention

Account and cloud-stored information may remain associated with your account while your Habito account is active or for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested services.

Local information may remain on your device until you delete it, clear application data, or uninstall Habito.

Certain information may be retained where required or permitted by law, for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or other legitimate purposes.

13. Account and Data Deletion

If Habito allows you to create an account, you may request deletion of your Habito account and associated personal data.

Account deletion requests may result in deletion of account-related information stored through Firebase Authentication and associated application data stored in Firestore, subject to information that we are legally required or permitted to retain.

To request account or data deletion, contact us using the email address provided below.

Important: If Habito provides account creation, Google Play requires a discoverable account-deletion path both inside the app and through an external web resource. The external resource must allow users to initiate an account deletion request.

14. Children's Privacy

Habito is intended for a general audience and is not knowingly designed to collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through Habito, please contact us so that we can review the request and take appropriate action.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including:

  • Access to personal information
  • Correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion of applicable information
  • Withdrawal of consent where applicable
  • Information about how personal data is processed
  • The ability to submit privacy-related complaints

You may contact us to exercise applicable privacy rights.

16. India Data Protection

Habito is developed with consideration for applicable Indian data protection requirements, including the Digital Personal Data Protection framework to the extent applicable to our processing activities.

We aim to collect and process personal information only for legitimate and relevant purposes and to provide transparency about our processing practices.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable service terms.

We recommend reviewing Google's privacy information for more details about Google's processing practices.
